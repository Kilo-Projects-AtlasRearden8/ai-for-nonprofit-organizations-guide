Donor thank-you notes with free AI

A good thank-you note keeps donors giving. AI can write the first draft in seconds. Your personal touch makes it worth reading.

Why this matters

  • 70% of first-time donors never give again — a personal thank-you changes that.
  • A prompt thank-you is better than none. Take 5 minutes.
  • Handwritten or personally-signed notes stand out against mass emails.

The workflow

  1. Collect facts for each donor — name, gift amount and date, what the gift will do, any personal connection.
  2. Use a template with placeholders. Do not ask the AI to invent donor details.
  3. Fill in each donor and read it out loud. Adjust tone per donor.
  4. Send — email for most, handwritten for major donors and volunteers.

Prompt template

Write a warm, sincere thank-you note for a donor. Use only these facts.

Donor: [name]
Gift: $[amount] on [date]
Impact: [one specific thing the gift provides]
Tone: [warm / formal / casual]
Length: 100-150 words
Do not invent facts or relationships.

Privacy note

Donor names and gift amounts are private. Use a local model. Do not paste a donor list into any hosted service.
