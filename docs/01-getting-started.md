# 1. Getting Started: AI for Nonprofits, Plain English

Welcome. This guide explains how to use AI to help with the four tasks most nonprofits need: **grant letters, donor thank-you notes, event flyers, and volunteer schedules.**

You do not need to be a tech person. You do not need to pay for anything. You do not need to share your donors' private data with anyone.

## What AI is (in one sentence)

Artificial intelligence is a computer program trained on millions of examples. Give it a clear instruction, and it can write, organize, and format things for you — like a very fast, very patient assistant who never gets tired.

## What AI is good at (for nonprofits)

- Turning bullet points into full paragraphs.
- Rewriting the same message in different tones (formal, warm, urgent).
- Formatting text into tables, lists, and layouts.
- Checking your work for typos and clarity.
- Brainstorming ideas when you are stuck.

## What AI is *not* good at

- Knowing your donors, your mission, or your budget without you telling it.
- Being accurate on its own. It guesses. **You check.**
- Handling secrets. Never give it private donor data unless you have consent.

## The golden rule

> **Draft with AI. Edit with your heart.**

AI produces a first draft. Your knowledge, your relationships, and your judgment make it real. Always read, personalize, and fix everything before it goes out.

## The three-step workflow

1. **Prompt** — Tell the AI what you want, in plain English. Give it context (who, what, when, tone).
2. **Review** — Read the draft. Fix names, amounts, dates, and facts.
3. **Polish** — Make it yours. Add the personal details that only you know.
