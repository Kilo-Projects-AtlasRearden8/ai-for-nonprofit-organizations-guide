Event flyers with free AI

Flyers are copy and layout. AI handles the copy fast; you handle the layout and the design rules.

What AI can do

  • Write event copy (title, hook, details, call to action).
  • Suggest headline variants.
  • Organize details into a clean list.
  • Rewrite copy at different lengths.

What AI cannot do

  • Design the actual layout.
  • Know your branding unless you tell it.
  • Produce a print-ready file by itself.

The workflow

  1. Gather your facts — event name, audience, date, time, location, what happens, how to register, contact.
  2. Write the core copy with a prompt using only those facts.
  3. Get headline variants — 5 short options.
  4. Layout with a free tool — Canva, Google Slides, or LibreOffice Draw.
  5. Check before you print — date, time, location, registration link, contact email.

Prompt template

I am writing a flyer for a community event. Use only these facts.

Event: [name]
Audience: [who it is for]
Date: [date]
Time: [time]
Location: [address or link]
What happens: [list]
How to register: [instructions]
Contact: [name and email]

Write flyer copy with these parts:
1. Headline (short, catchy)
2. Sub-headline (one sentence)
3. A short description (50-80 words)
4. Bullet list of key details
5. A call to action (how to sign up)

Write in plain, warm English. Do not invent facts, dates, times, or locations.

Design rules

  • One message per flyer.
  • Date, time, and location in large, bold text.
  • One clear call to action.
  • High contrast (dark text on light background).

Privacy note

Flyers are public by design. Do not put anyone's personal phone number or email on a flyer unless they have agreed.
