Volunteer schedules with free AI

Scheduling volunteers is half organization, half people-work. AI helps with the organization. You handle the people part.

The workflow

  1. Gather your facts — dates, shifts, volunteers needed per shift, availability, role requirements.
  2. Give the AI a clean table input — shifts and volunteer availability.
  3. Check the schedule — every shift filled, no one scheduled outside availability, fair distribution.
  4. Send reminders — draft reminder messages.
  5. Handle changes — rebalance when someone cancels.

Prompt template

Build a volunteer schedule from these facts. Do not invent volunteers.

Event: [name]
Dates: [date range]

Shifts needed:
- [Shift name] | [start] | [end] | volunteers needed: [N]

Volunteers and their availability:
- [Name] | [available shifts]

Rules:
- Each shift must have the number of volunteers listed above.
- Do not schedule a volunteer for a shift they cannot work.
- Spread shifts fairly.

Output a table: Date | Shift | Time | Volunteers assigned.
Then list any shifts still unfilled.

Privacy note

Volunteer names, contact details, and availability are private. Use a local model. Do not paste a volunteer list into a hosted service.
